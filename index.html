Johnny E (John Emslie)

Composer · Sound Designer · Audio Technologist

I create music and sound for film, games, and unusual worlds. My work brings together composition, production, and sound design, with a fascination for atmosphere, tension, and the unfamiliar.

My background spans recording, live audiovisual production, game audio, and professional music technology. I hold an MA in Sound in New Media from Aalto University and work at Neural DSP supporting musicians and their audio tools.

Alongside this, I’m developing Python-based audio tools and exploring synthesis, signal processing, and interactive sound. I’m interested in how sound gives a world its character — and makes it stay with you.
